Help with Sudoku Galaxies

Found a bug, have a question, or have an idea? Email jamisonkimminau@gmail.com.

How do I get the full version?

Go to Play and tap Endless Mode, or any locked Level, then tap Unlock. It's a single purchase, with no subscription.

I bought the full version, but it's locked on my new phone

Open the full version screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can my family use my purchase?

Yes. The full version supports Family Sharing, so members of your Family Sharing group can unlock it for free with Restore Purchase.

The Unlock button says "Try Again"

The app couldn't reach the App Store. Check your internet connection and tap it again.

Will my progress carry over to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and included in your iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings it along. Deleting the app erases its progress.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and find the purchase.
