Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

In short: Sudoku Galaxies doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything the app keeps stays on your iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your game progress, including puzzles in progress, completed stages, medals, and times
  • Your streaks, statistics, and unlocked themes
  • Your settings

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app can remind you to keep your daily streak going. These reminders are scheduled on your device and don't involve any server.

Purchases

The full version is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple tells the app only whether the full version has been purchased. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
